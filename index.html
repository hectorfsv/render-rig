How to run it

  1. Pick Video or Image.
  2. Say what you want in plain English. Don't write like a robot — Grok rewrites your line into a full brief before the model ever sees it.
  3. Optional: add a photo to start from.
  4. Check the price on the meter, then hit Generate.
  5. Leave the tab open. When it lands you get a player and a Download button.
