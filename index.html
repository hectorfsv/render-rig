What this thing is

Three jobs, one rig. Video makes a clip with Kling 3.0 Pro. Image makes a still with Krea or Nano Banana Pro. Upscale enlarges a picture you already have, using Topaz.
You only pay for output that actually arrives. A refusal, a crash or a run you abandon costs nothing. fal bills on delivery.
Whatever you write gets rewritten by Grok into a fuller brief before the model sees it, so plain English beats keyword soup. Say what you want, not what you think a model wants to hear.

Seed

Every render starts from a random number. That number is the seed, and it decides all the arbitrary choices the model makes: which way a face turns, where the light falls, how a crowd is arranged.
Same seed + same prompt = the same picture, every time. Change the seed and you get a completely different take on the same description.
That is what the lock is for. Run something you like, lock the seed, then change one word in the prompt. Whatever moves is what that word actually did. Without a lock you change a word, get a different picture, and never know whether it was your edit or the dice.
The dice button rolls a new number by hand. Leave the field empty and every run rolls its own.
Krea ignores the seed. It is a Nano Banana Pro feature only.

Which image model

Krea 2 Large$0.06Text only. Will draw real people, actors and copyrighted characters without complaint.
Nano Banana Pro$0.15Sharper, reads the source tray, obeys the seed. Refuses celebrities and anything trademarked.
The split is capability, not quality tiers. Krea is not the cheap option, it is the unfiltered one. Nano Banana Pro is the one that can combine your photos and repeat a look on demand.
If Nano Banana Pro refuses your subject, Krea is the fallback and it costs less. If Krea gives you something soft or generic, Nano Banana Pro is the fix.
Nothing unfiltered beats Krea at this price. That was measured, not assumed, so there is no better model waiting to be found.

Sources

Anything in the source tray is handed to Nano Banana Pro, which merges every one of them into a single picture. Give it a face, a jacket and a room and ask for all three together.
Order matters to the model, and the tray is numbered so you can see it. Describe them in your prompt the same way — "the man in image 1 wearing the coat from image 2".
Krea ignores the tray entirely. The cost meter will tell you when you have sources loaded and Krea selected.
Video only uses the first source, as the opening frame.
Sources are shrunk to 1536px before they are sent, so several photos stay quick to upload. Upscale is the one exception — it sends the original untouched.

Resolution

2K$0.15The default, and the right answer nearly always.
4K$0.30Double the price. Worth it only if the picture is going to print or a big screen.
There used to be a 1K button. It was removed because fal charges the same for 1K and 2K, which made it a button that could only ever cost you detail for free.

Reckon on about 44 seconds of rendering for every 1 second of video. A 10 second clip takes roughly 7 minutes.
Add a photo and the video copies that photo's shape. The aspect ratio buttons stop doing anything; the picture decides. If you want a 9:16 clip, start from a 9:16 image.
The sound is written from your words. A prompt with no sound cues comes back nearly silent. Name the song, the line of dialogue, the room tone — whatever you want to hear, say it.
Kling will sometimes ignore a camera instruction, an orbit especially. That is the model, not the rig, and it does it on every speed setting.

Upscale

Up to 24 MP$0.08A 2K picture enlarged 2× lands here.
Up to 48 MP$0.162K at 3×.
Up to 96 MP$0.322K at 4×.
Runs on Topaz. The price is set by the size of what comes out, so the meter works out the exact figure from your source instead of guessing at it.
Topaz is both the cheapest and the most capable option here. The obvious alternative, Clarity, bills per megapixel and would charge about $0.50 for the same job.
Pick the model to match the source. Standard for photographs, High Fidelity when there is fine detail worth protecting, Low Resolution for something small or soft, CGI for renders and illustration, Text Refine for screenshots and type, Recovery for something damaged or heavily compressed.
Faces are enhanced automatically. Upscale uses the first source and needs no prompt.

Working in the console

The stage keeps your last result while you set up the next run, so you can look at what you got while you change what you asked for.
Everything you make drops into the session strip. Tap one to bring it back to the stage; the small arrow sends it back into the source tray so you can build on it, upscale it, or use it as a video's opening frame.
The session strip lives in memory only. A refresh loses it. Download anything you want to keep.
Keep the tab open while something renders. The job keeps running on fal either way, but this page is how you get the file.
